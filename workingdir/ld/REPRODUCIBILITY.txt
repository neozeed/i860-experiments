   python3 tools/build_ld860_r1.py /path/to/original/ld860 --outdir artifacts --builddir build

2. Independent replay:
   python3 probe_link_r1.py

   6ae563bf61ad6f5f2aab662b92b6c9b7257e9a42d485ab4bdacef1334febad09

   85b2531a8501e67731703cc2856515080338795d221b00c2985e83298e277528

The builder uses 32-bit freestanding GCC/assembler/linker objects and emits the PE deterministically. A second clean build was byte-identical.
